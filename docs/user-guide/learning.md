# How learning works

nameIT keeps a small learning record per student and uses it to decide whom
you see next. You don't have to manage anything – just play.

## Five levels

Every student has a **level from 0 to 5** – the five dots under their photo.

| Answer | Effect |
|---|---|
| Correct | level **+1** (up to 5) |
| Wrong | level **−2** (down to 0) |

A wrong answer costs more than a right one earns, so a name only counts as
known once you get it right several times in a row.

The class bar groups the levels into three colours:

| Colour | Levels | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| <span style="opacity:.6">grey</span> | 0 | **new** – never right yet, or forgotten again |
| amber | 1 – 4 | **learning** |
| mint | 5 | **known** |

## Who comes up next

Games don't pick students at random. Each student gets a weight:

* the **lower the level**, the more likely they appear, and
* the **longer you haven't seen them** (up to a week), the more likely too.

So weak names come up often, known names still come back from time to time,
and nobody is forgotten. This is a simple form of
[spaced repetition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaced_repetition) (the
levels work like the boxes of a
[Leitner system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leitner_system)).

Wrong options in multiple-choice games are other students **of the same
class** – the names you would actually confuse.

## What counts

| Game | What is recorded |
|---|---|
| Millionaire, Who is it?, Reveal, Type it, Blitz | every answer |
| Memory | a pair found without a wrong match before counts as correct |
| Flashcards | only your **first** rating of each card per round |

## XP and streak

* Correct answers give **XP** – how much depends on the game (Reveal pays
  more for early guesses). XP count per **school year** (1 August to
  31 July); the home screen shows this school year's.
* Answer at least one question on a day and your **streak** goes up. Miss a
  day and it starts again at 1.

Both are shown on the home screen and in [Stats](stats.md), where you can
also look back at earlier school years. The streak doesn't reset with a new
school year.

## Starting over

**Settings → Reset learning progress** sets every student back to level 0
and clears XP, streak, history and badges – for all school years. Classes and
photos stay.
